How to Set Your Location
1
From Google Maps link
Open Google Maps → go to your office → right-click the building → click the coordinates at the top to copy → paste in the box on the left.
OR: share the maps link (like maps.google.com/maps?q=10.06,76.34) and paste it directly.
2
Type coordinates
Copy the numbers from Google Maps (e.g. 10.062903, 76.347862) and type them in the Latitude and Longitude boxes.
3
Use current GPS
If you are physically at the office, click "Use My Current GPS Location" — the browser will capture your position automatically. Allow location permission when asked.
